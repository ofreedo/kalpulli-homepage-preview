About Us

Kalpulli Ehecacoatl, nestled in the vibrant community of Santa Cruz County, particularly in Watsonville, extends its cultural embrace to neighboring areas like Pajaro. As stewards of Aztec heritage, we proudly offer immersive dance classes for all those seeking to delve into the rich tapestry of our tradition. Our rhythmic beats and colorful regalia enhance private gatherings and community celebrations, highlighting the enduring presence of our indigenous culture. We welcome you to be part of our mission to create a nurturing environment where learning flourishes and our ancestral legacy shines brightly.

Mission & Vision

The mission of Kalpulli Ehecacoatl

Mission
To offer a safe and inclusive space, where each member is essential to strengthen our community and teach traditions to future generations through dance, music and culture.
Vision
To preserve the traditions of indigenous cultures from Mexico and create positive and healthy change in the community.
Our Name

"Kalpulli Ehecacoatl" is a name rich in meaning

Derived from Nahuatl concepts, our name combines "Kalpulli" — a learning community or a place of ceremonial gathering — with "Ehecacoatl," meaning serpent of the wind. "Ehecatl" (wind) represents our first breath at birth and symbolizes abundance, as the wind is the force that brings life-giving rain clouds. "Coatl" (serpent) represents movement, transformation, and knowledge.
