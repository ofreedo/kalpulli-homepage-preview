Book Us

Learn More

¡Vive la belleza de nuestra cultura!

Book us for private gatherings, celebrations, festivals, cultural events, school events, and assemblies. Our performances feature full regalia dancing and traditional drumming.

Available Services

We offer unique performances and educational programs to enhance your event or community gathering.

Cultural Dance Performances

We perform at private gatherings, community festivals, cultural events, quinceañeras, birthdays, special occasions, schools, elementary and university events, and assemblies.

Educational Demonstrations

Traditional "Pre-Colombian" dance and music presentations suitable for schools or community groups.

Community Engagement & Outreach

We commit to fostering connections through demonstrations and cultural exchanges, promoting understanding and appreciation of indigenous heritage.

Booking Considerations

What to know before you book

Lead Time

Because we are a volunteer-based collective, it is best to contact us at least 4–6 weeks in advance, especially for weekend events.

Cultural Context

When booking, it is helpful to provide details on the intent and purpose of your event to ensure it aligns with our mission.
