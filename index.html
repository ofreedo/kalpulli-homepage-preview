Our Mission & Vision

A safe, inclusive space to keep tradition alive

The mission of Kalpulli Ehecacoatl is to offer a safe and inclusive space, where each member is essential to strengthen our community and teach traditions to future generations through dance, music, and culture — preserving the traditions of indigenous cultures from Mexico and creating a positive, healthy change in the community.

“Kalpulli Ehecacoatl” is a name rich in meaning, drawn from Nahuatl.

Kalpulli
A learning community — a place of ceremonial gathering.
Ehecatl — Wind
Our first breath at birth. The force that brings life-giving rain.
Coatl — Serpent
Movement, transformation, and knowledge.
