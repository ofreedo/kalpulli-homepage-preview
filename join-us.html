Aztec Dance Classes

Join Us

Everyone is welcome to join our Aztec dance classes. Our classes are free of charge and no prior experience is necessary.

Class Schedule

Mondays & Thursdays
6:30 PM – 9:00 PM

Location

All Saints Episcopal Church
437 Rogers Ave
Watsonville, CA 95076

What to Expect

A welcoming space to learn and grow

Each class begins with a beginner-friendly session where new participants learn the fundamentals of Aztec dance. The second portion is a more advanced practice focused on ceremonial formations and protocols. Whether you are taking your first steps or returning after time away, you'll find a welcoming, supportive, and inclusive community where you can learn, grow, and connect with Indigenous traditions.

Membership

Official members have access to

[Photo — Temazcal]

Temazcal

Our temazcal can be a healing and empowering experience for our dancers. Temazcal dates and times are announced at our dance classes.

[Photo — Workshops]

Regalia Workshops

After becoming an official member, participants can begin working on their regalia. Workshop dates and times are announced at our dance classes.

[Photo — Ceremonies]

Ceremonies

Members are encouraged to explore and learn from the rich Aztec dance community all over California. Together we visit the ceremonies held by different communities.

Ready to Join?
